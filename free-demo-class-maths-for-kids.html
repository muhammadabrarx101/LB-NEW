Free maths demo class for kids

A live, playful 30-minute maths session with tricks, puzzles and patterns — designed to show children that maths can be fun and that they can do it.

Book this free demo

What happens in the 30 minutes

  1. A quick puzzle to see how your child approaches problems.
  2. A mental maths trick that makes a hard-looking sum easy.
  3. Pattern spotting — the habit behind confident maths.
  4. Your questions about the course and how we keep it fun.

Who it suits

Children aged 6–10, especially those who find maths stressful or boring. The aim is confidence first.

What to prepare

  • A notebook and pencil
  • A laptop or tablet with a camera and a quiet spot
  • A parent nearby for the first few minutes for younger children

The demo is a slice of our Math Magic course.

What happens after the demo

You get an honest view of where the student is and what would help — including if we are not the right fit. If you want to continue, we share schedules, group options and fees on WhatsApp. If not, that is the end of it: no card details, no payment and no follow-up pressure.

How to book

Use the booking button on this page, or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. Tell us the student's age and which days and times suit you, and we will reply with two or three slots — usually within a few hours. See all free demo classes.

Frequently asked questions

Is this demo for school maths or exam maths?
It is for ages 6–10 and focuses on confidence, mental maths and problem-solving. For IGCSE or O Level Maths, book the IGCSE demo instead.

My child is anxious about maths. Will this help?
The session is playful and pressure-free, designed to build confidence rather than test.

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. It is free, takes about 30 minutes and needs no card details or payment.

Who teaches the demo?
The teacher who would actually take the course, so you see the real teaching style.
