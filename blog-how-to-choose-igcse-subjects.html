How to choose IGCSE and O Level subjects

Subject choices at 14 or 15 shape A-Level and university options later. The aim is to keep the right doors open without overloading the student.

Quick answer

Start with required subjects, add the sciences and Maths your child may need for future paths, then choose subjects they enjoy and do well in. Keep the total manageable — more subjects is not automatically better.

Step 1: check what is required

Schools in Pakistan commonly require English, Maths, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies (for O Level students), and many require at least one or two sciences. Check your school's rules and current IBCC equivalence requirements.

Step 2: think about future paths

Possible future pathSubjects that usually help
Medicine, dentistry, pharmacyBiology, Chemistry, often Physics and Maths
EngineeringPhysics, Maths, Chemistry
Computer scienceMaths, Physics, Computer Science if offered
Business, finance, accountingMaths, Economics, Accounting, Business Studies
Law, humanities, social sciencesEnglish, History or Sociology, Economics

If your child is unsure, keeping Maths and at least two sciences keeps many options open.

Step 3: add subjects they enjoy

Motivation matters. A subject a student likes usually ends with a better grade than one chosen only because it "looks good".

Step 4: watch the workload

More subjects means more coursework, more revision and less depth. Universities generally care more about strong grades in relevant subjects than about the sheer number taken.

Questions to ask the school

  • Which subjects are compulsory?
  • What combinations are allowed in the timetable?
  • Are subjects offered at IGCSE or O Level?
  • What are the entry requirements for A-Level in each subject?

Changing your mind later

Changing subjects is easiest early in the course. If a choice is clearly not working, talk to the school quickly rather than waiting.

Frequently asked questions

How many IGCSE subjects should my child take?
Enough to meet requirements and keep future options open, without overload. Strong grades in relevant subjects usually matter more than the number.

Which O Level subjects are needed for medicine?
Biology and Chemistry are usually essential, often with Physics and Maths. Check the requirements of the programmes you are interested in.

Are Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies compulsory?
For many O Level students in Pakistan they are required by schools and for equivalence. Check your school's and IBCC's current rules.

Can I change IGCSE subjects later?
It is easiest early in the course. Talk to the school quickly if a choice is not working.
