Family AI rules poster: an AI safety activity for kids

Rules children help write are rules children remember. Make a poster together that sets out how your family uses AI tools safely.

Lectures about safety are quickly forgotten. A poster your child designed, with rules they helped choose, is something they actually look at.

  • Ages: 7–14
  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Skill: Safe and responsible AI use

What you need

  • A large sheet of paper or card
  • Coloured pens, pencils or paints
  • Optional: stickers or pictures

Step by step

  1. Talk first. Ask your child where they use or see AI, and what could go wrong.
  2. Agree five rules. For example: never share personal details; check important facts; AI explains, I do the work; tell an adult if something feels wrong; it is software, not a friend.
  3. Write them clearly. One rule per line, in your child’s words.
  4. Illustrate each rule. A small drawing next to each makes it memorable.
  5. Sign it. Everyone in the family signs the poster — adults too.
  6. Put it up. Stick it near where devices are used.

Rules worth including

  • Never share name, school, address, photos or passwords
  • Check important facts in a book or trusted site
  • AI explains — I do the work for homework
  • Tell a trusted adult if anything feels strange
  • AI is software, not a person or a friend

What your child learns

Practical, memorable habits for using AI safely and honestly. Read is AI safe for kids? and digital safety for kids.
