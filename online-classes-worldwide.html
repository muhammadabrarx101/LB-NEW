Online classes worldwide — wherever your family lives

Learning Bubble is based in Pakistan and teaches students around the world live online, in English, at times that fit each family's time zone.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble teaches live online classes worldwide. Families in Pakistan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, China, Japan, Singapore and Malaysia join our Kids courses — coding, AI, creative writing and maths — and our Academics programmes — IGCSE, A-Level, IELTS and SAT. Every class is taught live in English, in small groups or one-to-one, and timed for your own time zone. Start with a free demo class.

Frequently asked questions

Which countries do you teach students in?
Students join from Pakistan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, China, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia and other countries. Classes are online, so anywhere with a stable internet connection works.

How do you handle different time zones?
We schedule each group or one-to-one class in the student's own time zone, usually after school or at weekends. Exact slots are agreed when you enrol.

Are all classes in English?
Yes. All Learning Bubble classes are taught live in English.

How much do the classes cost?
Fees depend on the course, small group or one-to-one, and the number of sessions, so we quote them personally. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610 or book a free demo for the exact fee.

Is there a free trial class?
Yes. Every course starts with a free 30-minute demo class, with no obligation to enrol.
