Free A-Level demo class

Thirty minutes with an A-Level subject specialist: a short diagnostic, specification-mapped teaching and a look at the command words that decide long-answer marks.

Book this free demo

What happens in the 30 minutes

  1. Subject, board and level — AS or A2, and your exam session.
  2. A short topic diagnostic to find the real gaps.
  3. Teaching mapped to the specification, on one weak topic.
  4. Exam technique — command words and structuring long answers.

Subjects

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Further Maths, Economics, Business, Accounting and English Literature. If your subject is not listed, ask — we will tell you honestly whether we can teach it well.

What to prepare

  • A laptop or computer with a camera and headphones
  • Your exam board and syllabus code
  • A recent mock or test result, if you have one

See all A-Level programmes.

What happens after the demo

You get an honest view of where the student is and what would help — including if we are not the right fit. If you want to continue, we share schedules, group options and fees on WhatsApp. If not, that is the end of it: no card details, no payment and no follow-up pressure.

How to book

Use the booking button on this page, or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. Tell us the student's age and which days and times suit you, and we will reply with two or three slots — usually within a few hours. See all free demo classes.

The teacher who would actually take the course, so you see the real teaching style.
