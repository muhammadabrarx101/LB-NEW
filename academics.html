The method

How we move a grade

The same four stages, whatever the exam.

Diagnose

A topic-wise assessment before any teaching. We need to know precisely where the marks are being lost — not a general impression.

Rebuild concepts

We fix the underlying understanding first. Exam technique on top of a shaky concept collapses under pressure.

Drill past papers

Full cycles against the real mark scheme, learning the command words and exactly what examiners reward.

Time and track

Timed mocks with performance tracking, so the final weeks are about pace and accuracy rather than panic.

Why students switch to us

No group larger than five. Ever.

Most tuition centres solve their economics by filling a room. We would rather keep the group small enough that the teacher knows, in every single session, which student has not understood the last five minutes.

  • Teachers who specialise in one syllabus, not all of them
  • Written feedback on every submitted answer
  • Sessions recorded for revision
  • Pacing matched to your school and exam session

Before you book

We teach to the major international boards for IGCSE and A-Level. Tell us your board and syllabus code in your enquiry and we will confirm before you commit to anything.

Groups of 3–5 work well for most students and add useful peer discussion. One-to-one is better when you are starting late, have very specific gaps, or need an unusual schedule. We will recommend one after the diagnostic rather than upselling by default.

For IGCSE and A-Level, four to six months before the exam session is comfortable. The SAT programme is designed as four months. IELTS can be done in as little as three to four weeks if you are already close to your target band.
