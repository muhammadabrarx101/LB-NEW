Free coding demo class for kids

Thirty minutes, live online. Your child writes their first lines of real code, sees something appear on screen, and meets the teacher who would take the course.

Book this free demo

What happens in the 30 minutes

  1. A quick chat about what your child enjoys and any coding they have tried.
  2. First code. Your child writes a few lines of real code and sees a shape or animation appear on screen.
  3. A small challenge: changing the code to make something new happen.
  4. Your questions about the course, the schedule and what comes next.

Who it suits

Children aged 10–16, including complete beginners. Younger children can warm up with our free at-home coding activities.

What to prepare

  • A laptop or desktop computer (not a phone)
  • A stable connection and headphones
  • We send any setup instructions in advance

The demo is a slice of our Fun with Coding course, which uses Small Basic before students move on to Python.

What happens after the demo

You get an honest view of where the student is and what would help, including if we are not the right fit. If you want to continue, we share schedules, group options and fees on WhatsApp. If not, that is the end of it: no card details, no payment and no follow-up pressure.

How to book

Use the booking button on this page, or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. Tell us the student's age and which days and times suit you, and we will reply with two or three slots, usually within a few hours. See all free demo classes.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need any coding experience?
No. The coding demo is designed for complete beginners.

Can the demo be done on a tablet?
A laptop or desktop is strongly recommended, because coding needs a keyboard and installed software.

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. It is free, takes about 30 minutes and needs no card details or payment.

Who teaches the demo?
The teacher who would actually take the course, so you see the real teaching style.
