SAT Reading and Writing tips for the digital SAT

Fifty-four short questions in 64 minutes. Each question has its own passage, which changes how you read — and which mistakes cost the most.

Quick answer

The Reading and Writing section has 54 questions in two 32-minute modules. Every question has its own short passage, so you have a little over a minute per question. Read the question first, then read the passage with that question in mind.

The four question areas

AreaWhat it testsExample
Craft and StructureWord meaning, structure, how two texts relate"Which word most logically completes the text?"
Information and IdeasCentral ideas, evidence, inference, data"Which finding would most directly support the claim?"
Standard English ConventionsGrammar and punctuation"Which choice conforms to the conventions of Standard English?"
Expression of IdeasTransitions and achieving a goal"Which transition completes the text most logically?"

Grammar that comes up again and again

  • Subject–verb agreement, especially with long phrases between subject and verb
  • Punctuation that joins clauses — commas, semicolons, colons and dashes
  • Pronoun agreement and clear pronoun reference
  • Verb tense consistency
  • Modifiers — making sure the descriptive phrase sits next to what it describes

Convention questions are the most learnable on the test. A small set of rules, practised properly, turns them into reliable points.

Transitions

Decide the relationship between the two sentences before you look at the options: is it contrast, cause, addition or example? Then pick the word that matches. Choosing by "sound" is how students fall for the trap options.

Evidence and inference

The correct answer is supported by the text — not just reasonable. If you need to add your own assumption to make an option work, it is probably wrong.

Timing

Do not let one hard question eat three minutes. Answer, flag it in Bluebook and come back at the end of the module. There is no penalty for wrong answers, so never leave a question blank.

Build reading stamina anyway

The passages are short, but they cover science, history, literature and social studies. Reading quality nonfiction regularly — articles, essays, explanations — builds the vocabulary and comprehension that no shortcut replaces.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are in SAT Reading and Writing?
54 questions across two 32-minute modules.

Are there long passages on the digital SAT?
No. Each question has its own short passage, usually one paragraph or a short extract.

Is grammar tested on the SAT?
Yes. Standard English Conventions questions test grammar and punctuation, and they are among the most learnable on the test.

Is there a penalty for wrong answers?
No. Always answer every question.
