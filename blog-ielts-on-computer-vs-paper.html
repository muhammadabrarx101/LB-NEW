IELTS on computer vs paper: which format should you choose?

Same test, same marking, same certificate — but the experience is different. The right choice depends on how you type, how you read and how soon you need results.

Quick answer

Both formats have the same questions, timing, marking and certificate. Computer-delivered results usually arrive within a few days; paper results take longer. Choose computer if you type comfortably; choose paper if you write faster by hand and like annotating texts.

What is exactly the same

  • The content and difficulty of every module
  • The marking criteria and band scores
  • The Speaking test, which is always face-to-face with an examiner
  • The certificate, which does not say which format you took

What is different

ComputerPaper
ListeningType answers as you go; short review time at the endWrite on the question paper; extra time to transfer answers
ReadingHighlight on screen; passage and questions side by sideUnderline and annotate freely on paper
WritingType, with a live word countHandwrite and count words yourself
ResultsUsually within a few daysUsually takes longer
AvailabilityOften more test datesDepends on the centre

Choose computer-delivered if

  • You type faster and more neatly than you write
  • You want the automatic word count in Writing
  • You need results quickly for an application deadline
  • You are comfortable reading long texts on screen

Choose paper-based if

  • You write quickly by hand and your typing is slow
  • You like to underline and make notes on Reading passages
  • Screens tire your eyes over long periods

A common worry: spelling

On computer there is no spellcheck, so spelling mistakes still cost marks. Typing does not make you a better speller — practise spelling either way.

Practise in the format you will sit

The skills transfer, but the habits do not. If you will sit the computer test, do your timed practice on a screen so that scrolling, highlighting and typing feel normal on test day.

Frequently asked questions

Is computer IELTS easier than paper IELTS?
No. The questions, timing and marking are the same. Some people simply find one format more comfortable.

Is the Speaking test on a computer?
No. In both formats the Speaking test is a face-to-face conversation with a certified examiner.

How fast are computer-delivered IELTS results?
Usually within a few days of the test, which is faster than paper. Your test centre will confirm the exact timeline.

Does the certificate show which format I took?
No. The certificate is the same whichever format you choose.
