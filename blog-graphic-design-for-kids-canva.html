Graphic design for kids: getting started with Canva

Good design is a skill, not a talent. A few simple rules — layout, colour, fonts and what to make biggest — turn a messy poster into one that looks designed.

Quick answer

Children can start graphic design from about 10 with a tool like Canva. The fastest improvement comes from four basics: layout, colour, fonts and hierarchy (what the eye sees first). Once those feel natural, tools like Illustrator open up more control.

The four basics

BasicSimple rule
HierarchyMake the most important thing the biggest
LayoutLine things up; leave empty space around them
ColourUse two or three colours that work together
FontsUse one or two fonts — not five

Most beginner designs improve instantly by removing things rather than adding them.

First projects that work

  1. A school project cover page — title, name, one image
  2. A birthday invitation — practising hierarchy (what, when, where)
  3. A poster for a school event — using a strong headline
  4. Presentation slides — consistent colours and fonts across slides
  5. A personal logo — simple shapes and lettering

Canva or Illustrator?

Canva is template-based and quick to learn — ideal for beginners and school work. Illustrator is a professional vector tool with more control and a steeper learning curve, better for teenagers ready to create from scratch. Starting in Canva and moving to Illustrator works well for most students.

Templates: help or crutch?

Templates are a good way to learn what works. The step up is understanding why a template works — and then making your own design without one.

Why design is a useful skill

School presentations, project boards, social media for clubs, and later university and work presentations all benefit. Design also teaches visual communication: getting a message across quickly and clearly.

Frequently asked questions

What age can kids start graphic design?
From around 10, using beginner-friendly tools such as Canva.

Is Canva good for kids?
Yes. It is easy to learn and useful for school projects. Children should still learn the design principles behind templates.

Should my child learn Canva or Illustrator?
Start with Canva for the basics, then move to Illustrator when they want more control and are ready for a professional tool.

What will my child make in a design course?
Posters, invitations, presentation designs and simple logos — building a small portfolio of their own work.
