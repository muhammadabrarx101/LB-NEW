Online classes in Karachi — kids courses, O Level, IELTS and SAT from home

Skip the traffic. Learning Bubble teaches students across Karachi live online, in small groups or one-to-one, at times that fit school and family.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble is an online academy, so students in any part of Karachi — from Clifton and DHA to Gulshan, North Nazimabad and PECHS — join live classes from home. We teach O Level, IGCSE and A-Level, IELTS and SAT preparation, and creative and tech courses for kids such as coding, AI and creative writing. Every course starts with a free demo class.

Why Karachi families choose online classes

A 40-minute tuition centre lesson in Karachi can easily cost two hours once you add the drive there and back. Online classes remove that:

  • No commute — no traffic on Shahrah-e-Faisal, no waiting in the car, no rush after school
  • Small groups of 3–5 — more attention than a crowded tuition centre batch
  • Live teachers, never recordings — sessions are recorded afterwards so nothing is missed
  • Reschedule when exams or travel get in the way — common around mock season

O Level, IGCSE and A-Level tuition for Karachi students

Karachi has one of the largest Cambridge O Level and A-Level student communities in Pakistan, and most students sit papers in the May/June or October/November sessions. Our IGCSE and O Level tuition covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths, English, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies, Accounting, Economics and Business. Our A-Level tuition covers AS and A2 across the main science, maths and commerce subjects.

Every academics student starts with a short diagnostic, so lessons go where marks are actually being lost. See the full list on our IGCSE and O Level subjects page.

IELTS and SAT preparation in Karachi

Both IELTS and the SAT can be sat in Karachi — IELTS through the British Council and IDP test centres, and the SAT at College Board test centres in the city. Preparing for them does not need a classroom:

  • IELTS Academic for university admission abroad
  • IELTS General Training for work and migration
  • SAT preparation for the digital SAT, with timed practice

Not sure how long you need? Read how long IELTS preparation takes or our SAT preparation timeline.
