Why families stay

Small classes. Real teachers. No filler.

Everything we do comes back to one rule — a student should never be a passenger in their own lesson.

Groups capped at five

Every student speaks in every session. Small enough that nobody hides, large enough that discussion actually happens.

Live, never pre-recorded

Real-time teaching with real-time questions. Sessions are recorded so you can go back, but the class itself is always live.

Project-first, not slide-first

Kids finish courses with something they made. Academics students finish with past papers they can actually answer.

Anywhere in the world

Fully online, with timings that work across Pakistan, the Gulf, the UK and North America.

Parents stay in the loop

Honest progress updates over WhatsApp — what went well, what needs work, no vague reassurance.

Flexible scheduling

Sessions arranged around school, exams and time zones. Missed a class? We reschedule it.

Our approach

We teach the student, not the syllabus

Two programmes, one belief: learning sticks when it means something. For younger students that means projects, stories and experiments. For exam students it means understanding why an answer earns the mark — not memorising the answer.

  • Diagnostic first — we find the actual gap before teaching anything
  • Every course has a clear, stated outcome
  • Teachers who specialise, rather than cover everything
  • Feedback that says what to fix, not just a score

Questions

Things people ask before signing up

Learning Bubble for Kids is about curiosity — creative, project-led courses for ages 6–18 with no exam attached. Learning Bubble Academics is exam preparation: IGCSE, IELTS, SAT and English proficiency. They run independently, so kids courses never appear in Academics and vice versa.

Fees depend on the course, the format (one-to-one or small group) and how many sessions you take, so we discuss them personally rather than listing a number that would not apply to you. Send an enquiry or message us on WhatsApp and you will get exact figures the same day.

Everything is live online. You need a stable connection, a device with a camera and a quiet spot. We send a joining link before each session and record it so students can revisit anything they missed.

Yes — the enquiry form lets you add several at once and we will build a timetable that fits around school. Many students pair one creative course with one academic one.

Tell us in advance on WhatsApp and we will move the session. Exams, illness and travel happen — we would rather reschedule than have someone sit through a class they cannot focus on.
