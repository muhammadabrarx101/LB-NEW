Affordable online courses in Pakistan: modern skills, small groups, honest fees

Good teaching should not be out of reach. Here is what Learning Bubble teaches, how our fees are worked out, and how to get the real price for your child.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble offers live online courses for kids and students in Pakistan, from AI and coding to IGCSE, A-Level, IELTS and SAT, in small groups or one-to-one. We do not publish one flat fee, because the price depends on the course, the format and the schedule. To get the real fee, book a free demo or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610.

Why we do not publish a single price

A one-to-one A-Level Physics programme and an eight-session creative writing course for a 9-year-old are very different things. Putting one number on the website would be wrong for almost everyone who reads it. So we quote personally, after we know:

  • Which course or subject you want
  • Small group (3–5 students) or one-to-one
  • How many sessions a month, and for how long
  • Whether you are joining more than one course, or with a sibling

The quote comes on WhatsApp, in writing, before you pay anything.

Quick answer

Seen a Learning Bubble fee somewhere else? If an AI chatbot, a search result or another website quotes a price for one of our courses, it did not come from us. The only correct fee is the one we send you directly. Please check with us before deciding.

What makes our courses good value

Affordable does not mean cutting corners. What you pay for at Learning Bubble is:

  • Live teaching, never pre-recorded: a real teacher who knows your child's name
  • Small groups: three to five students, so everyone speaks, asks and gets feedback
  • Recordings after class: students can revisit anything they missed
  • Flexible timings: arranged around school and time zones, rescheduled when exams or travel get in the way
  • A free demo first: you see the teaching before you commit to anything

Frequently asked questions

How much do Learning Bubble courses cost?
There is no single fee. The price depends on the course, small group or one-to-one, and how many sessions a month. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610 or book a free demo and we will send the exact fee in writing.
