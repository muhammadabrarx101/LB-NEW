AI or not? A home scavenger hunt for kids

AI is all around the house, hidden in apps and devices. Hunting for it helps children notice how much they already use — and start asking how it works.

Most children use AI every day without realising it. This activity turns the house into a hunting ground and gets children asking the most important AI question: is this just following instructions, or is it learning from data?

  • Ages: 7–12
  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Skill: Recognising AI in everyday life

What you need

  • Paper and a pencil
  • Access to household devices with a parent

Step by step

  1. Make a checklist. Write "AI", "Not AI" and "Not sure" at the top of a page.
  2. Explore the house. Look at phones, TVs, speakers, apps, games, a calculator, a microwave, a light switch.
  3. Ask the key question. Does it learn from data or make predictions? Or does it just follow fixed instructions?
  4. Sort each item. Video recommendations and voice assistants: AI. A calculator or a light switch: not AI.
  5. Discuss the "not sure" list. Talk through the tricky ones together.
  6. Pick a favourite. Choose one AI example and guess what data it might have learned from.

Answers to expect

ItemAI?Why
Video or music recommendationsYesLearns from what you watch or listen to
Voice assistantYesRecognises speech and predicts answers
Phone face unlockYesLearned to recognise faces
CalculatorNoFollows fixed rules
Microwave timerNoFollows fixed instructions

What your child learns

To notice AI in everyday life and understand the difference between fixed instructions and learning from data — the first step in AI literacy.
