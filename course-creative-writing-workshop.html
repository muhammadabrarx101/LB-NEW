Creative Writing Workshop

A fast, generous jump-start for new writers.

Ages 10–182 daysAll levelsLive online · intensive

About this course

A two-day intensive covering the essentials of story: idea generation, structure, character and voice. Ideal for students who want to try creative writing before committing to a longer course.

What is included

  • Idea generation that works on demand
  • Structure and character basics
  • Live feedback on your draft
  • Perfect taster before a full course

How it runs

Live online · intensive — taught live, never pre-recorded, with sessions recorded afterwards so students can revisit anything they missed. Timings are arranged around school and time zones, and we reschedule when exams or travel get in the way.

Learning Bubble teaches online to students across Pakistan — Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad — as well as the Gulf, the UK and North America. You will need a laptop or tablet with a working camera and a reasonably stable connection. If a course requires any materials at home, we send the list before the first session.
