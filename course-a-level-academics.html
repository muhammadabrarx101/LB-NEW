About this programme

A-Level tuition focused on specification mastery and structured answering. We work through the syllabus in order, then move into full past-paper cycles — teaching students not just the content but exactly how examiners award marks. Available as small-group or one-to-one, across AS and A2.

What is included

  • Specification-mapped lesson sequence
  • Mark-scheme and command-word training
  • Structured long-answer technique
  • AS and A2 covered

Subjects covered

Physics Chemistry Biology Mathematics Further Mathematics Economics Business Accounting English Literature

Who this programme is for

Students in AS or A2 on Cambridge, Edexcel or another international board — whether they are aiming for top grades for university applications, catching up after a weak AS result, or preparing to retake a paper.

What you will be able to do

  • Work through the full specification for your subject in a clear order
  • Write structured long answers that match what the mark scheme rewards
  • Read command words correctly and plan answers before writing
  • Sit full timed papers and understand exactly where marks were lost

How the programme is structured

  1. Stage 1 — Diagnostic

    A topic-wise check at the start so lessons go where marks are actually being lost.

  2. Stage 2 — Specification teaching

    The syllabus taught in order, with the concepts behind each topic made clear before moving on.

  3. Stage 3 — Past-paper cycles

    Real past papers marked against the official mark scheme, with model answers and command-word practice.

  4. Stage 4 — Timed mocks

    Exam-condition practice in the final weeks, with progress tracked topic by topic.

How it runs

Small group (3–5) or 1-to-1 — taught live, never pre-recorded, with sessions recorded afterwards so students can revisit anything they missed. Timings are arranged around school and time zones, and we reschedule when exams or travel get in the way.

Learning Bubble teaches online to students across Pakistan — Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad — as well as the Gulf, the UK and North America.
