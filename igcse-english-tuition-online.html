IGCSE & O Level English tuition online

Live online English Language classes for Cambridge IGCSE First Language (0500), English as a Second Language (0510/0511) and O Level English (1123) — with detailed marked feedback on writing.

English is the subject where students improve least on their own, because they cannot see what is wrong with their own writing. Reading comprehension responds to technique; writing responds to repeated, specific feedback from someone who knows what each mark band rewards.

What the papers assess

Reading — understanding explicit and implied meaning, summary writing, and analysing how writers use language.

Directed writing — turning information from a text into a letter, speech, article or report for a specific audience.

Composition — descriptive and narrative (and in some papers argumentative) writing, marked for content, structure and accuracy.

Speaking and listening — part of some syllabuses, such as English as a Second Language 0511.

Which English does your child take?

First Language English (0500) is aimed at students whose strongest language is English. English as a Second Language (0510/0511) suits students who use English as an additional language. O Level English Language (1123) is the qualification many Pakistani schools enter students for. The skills overlap, but the papers and mark schemes differ, so we teach to the exact one.

How we teach IGCSE & O Level English

Every student starts with a topic-by-topic diagnostic, because "weak at English" is not specific enough to fix. We need to know which topics are losing marks, and whether the problem is understanding, recall or exam technique.

  1. Diagnose — a short test across the syllabus, marked against the real mark scheme.
  2. Rebuild concepts — we fix the underlying idea first. Technique on top of a shaky concept collapses under exam pressure.
  3. Drill past papers — full cycles of past papers, learning the command words and exactly what examiners give marks for.
  4. Time and track — timed papers in the final weeks, with progress tracked topic by topic.

Classes are live online in groups of 3–5 students, or one-to-one when a student is starting late or has very specific gaps. Sessions are recorded for revision, and parents get honest progress updates on WhatsApp.

Where students usually lose marks

  • Summary questions — copying phrases instead of rewording, or going over the word limit
  • Language analysis — naming a technique without explaining its effect
  • Directed writing — ignoring the audience, purpose or required format
  • Compositions — strong ideas let down by weak structure and paragraphing
  • Accuracy — tense consistency, articles and punctuation

Frequently asked questions

Which is easier, IGCSE First Language or ESL?
They are designed for different students rather than being easier or harder. ESL suits students who use English as an additional language. Your school decides the entry, and we teach to whichever paper your child sits.

Do you mark compositions and give feedback?
Yes. Writing is marked against the mark bands, with specific comments on what would move it up a band. That feedback is where most of the improvement comes from.

Do you teach O Level English 1123?
Yes. It is one of the most common English qualifications in Pakistan, and we use 1123 past papers and mark schemes.

Can tuition help with reading comprehension?
Yes. Comprehension responds quickly to technique: finding evidence, inferring meaning and writing answers in the format the mark scheme expects.
