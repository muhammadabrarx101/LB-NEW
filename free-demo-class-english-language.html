Free English language demo class

Thirty minutes of live English practice with a teacher: a quick placement check, real conversation with correction, and advice on the right course level.

What happens in the 30 minutes

  1. A placement check across speaking, listening, reading and writing.
  2. Live conversation on everyday or work topics, with specific corrections.
  3. Your goals — study, work, interviews, travel or confidence.
  4. A recommendation: Basic, Intermediate or Advanced.

Who it suits

Teenagers and adults who want to improve their English for study, work or daily life. Read CEFR levels explained and how to choose an online English speaking course.

What to prepare

  • A laptop or phone with a camera and headphones
  • A quiet place to speak freely

The demo leads into our English Language courses: Basic, Intermediate and Advanced.

What happens after the demo

You get an honest view of where the student is and what would help — including if we are not the right fit. If you want to continue, we share schedules, group options and fees on WhatsApp. If not, that is the end of it: no card details, no payment and no follow-up pressure.

How to book

Use the booking button on this page, or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. Tell us the student's age and which days and times suit you, and we will reply with two or three slots — usually within a few hours. See all free demo classes.

Yes, you will get a practical indication of your level and which course would suit you.

Mostly, yes, with a quick check of the other skills so the course recommendation is accurate.

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. It is free, takes about 30 minutes and needs no card details or payment.

The teacher who would actually take the course, so you see the real teaching style.
