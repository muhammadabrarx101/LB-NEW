Inside a class

What an hour actually looks like

Warm-up

A question, a puzzle or a strange fact. Five minutes to get everyone talking before anything serious starts.

The idea

One concept, explained through an example the class can hold on to. Never more than one idea per session.

Make something

The bulk of the hour. Writing, building, painting, coding, experimenting — with the teacher moving between students.

Share & go

Everyone shows what they did. Small challenge for the week, and out — no homework pile.

For parents

You will know exactly how it is going

No portals to log into, no reports full of adjectives. Just a message from the teacher on WhatsApp telling you what your child did, what they enjoyed, and what still needs work.

  • Safe, moderated sessions with a named teacher
  • Recordings shared so you can see the class yourself
  • Honest feedback, including when a course is not the right fit
  • Flexible rescheduling around school and exams
  • Screen time that produces something rather than consuming something
