Who we are

A small school with a stubborn belief

That nobody learns anything worth keeping while they are bored — and that this is true whether you are eight years old and writing your first story, or seventeen and three months out from your IGCSEs.

The story

It started with one class that would not end on time

Learning Bubble began as a handful of online sessions for children who found school flat. The sessions were small, the topics were odd — detective fiction, home experiments, mental-maths tricks — and the students kept asking to stay past the hour.

Word travelled, and soon parents were asking for something else entirely: help with IGCSEs, with IELTS, with the SAT. That is a different job. It needs structure, past papers and a clear-eyed view of where the marks are being lost.

So rather than blur the two, we split them. Learning Bubble for Kids stayed curious and project-led. Learning Bubble Academics became rigorous and exam-focused. Same care, two different jobs, run separately — which is why you will never see a Grade 10 Chemistry programme sitting next to a poetry workshop on this site.

Why we keep them separate

Curiosity and exam preparation need different pacing, different teachers and different measures of success. Mixing them serves neither well.

Learning Bubble for Kids

Ages 6–18 · No grades attached

Success here means a child voluntarily doing more than was asked. Sessions are short, hands-on and end with something made. We measure engagement, not marks.

What we hold to

Five things we do not compromise on

Small groups, always

Never more than five. If a group would need to grow to make the numbers work, we open a second group instead.

Honest feedback

Including the uncomfortable kind. If a course is the wrong fit or a target grade is not realistic in the time left, we say so.

No pressure selling

We do not chase, upsell or manufacture urgency. Ask your questions, take your time, decide when you are ready.

Understanding before technique

Exam tricks layered on top of a shaky concept fall apart under pressure. We fix the concept first, every time.

Safe, named teachers

You always know who is teaching your child. Sessions are moderated and recordings are shared with parents.

Flexibility as standard

Exams, illness and travel happen. Tell us and we move the session — no penalty, no argument.

How a student moves through

From first message to last session

  • You get in touch

    An enquiry through the site or a WhatsApp message. Tell us the age, the goal and anything that has not worked before.

  • We work out what is actually needed

    For Academics that means a topic-wise diagnostic. For Kids it means a short conversation about what the child is drawn to.

  • We propose a plan

    Course or subject, group or one-to-one, how many sessions and over what period — with exact fees for that specific plan.

  • Teaching starts

    Live sessions, recorded for revision, with regular messages to parents about how it is going.

  • We review, honestly

    Partway through we tell you what has improved and what has not, and adjust the plan rather than carrying on regardless.
