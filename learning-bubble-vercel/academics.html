The method

How we move a grade

The same four stages, whatever the exam.

Diagnose

A topic-wise assessment before any teaching. We need to know precisely where the marks are being lost — not a general impression.

Rebuild concepts

We fix the underlying understanding first. Exam technique on top of a shaky concept collapses under pressure.

Drill past papers

Full cycles against the real mark scheme, learning the command words and exactly what examiners reward.

Time and track

Timed mocks with performance tracking, so the final weeks are about pace and accuracy rather than panic.

Why students switch to us

No group larger than five. Ever.

Most tuition centres solve their economics by filling a room. We would rather keep the group small enough that the teacher knows, in every single session, which student has not understood the last five minutes.
