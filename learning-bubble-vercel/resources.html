Choosing the right thing

Kids or Academics?

If there is an exam with a date on it — IGCSE, IELTS, SAT — you want Academics. If the goal is curiosity, confidence or a skill for its own sake, you want Kids. Students often do one of each.

Compare the two

Picking by age

6–10 works best with short, playful sessions. 11–14 can handle longer projects and real tools. 15–18 benefits most from workshop-style courses with direct feedback.

See kids categories

When to start exam prep

IGCSE: four to six months before the session. SAT: the full four-month programme. IELTS: three to four weeks if you are already near your band, longer if not.

What you need at home

A laptop or tablet with a working camera, headphones, and a stable connection. Art and science courses use household materials — we send the list beforehand.

Using past papers properly

Doing papers is not the same as learning from them. Mark your own work against the scheme, log every lost mark by topic, and re-attempt only the questions you got wrong.

Getting a reluctant reader started

Start with a mystery rather than a classic. Plot pulls harder than prose at that age — which is exactly why our Sherlock Holmes course exists.
