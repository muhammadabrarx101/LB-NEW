Code Quest: free coding workshop for kids (ages 8–12)

A free, live coding workshop on Zoom where children write their first lines of code, find and fix a bug, and leave with a simple game they made themselves.

Register free

The fastest way to make a child believe they can code is to let them build something they can play. Code Quest skips the theory lecture and goes straight to a small game, introducing each idea at the moment it is needed.

What happens in the workshop

  1. Think like a programmer. Breaking a game idea into small steps a computer can follow.
  2. First lines of code. Writing and running code — and seeing something happen on screen.
  3. Make it a game. Adding movement, rules and a goal, one step at a time.
  4. Find and fix a bug. Every programmer debugs. Children learn to read what went wrong and fix it.
  5. Show and play. Children leave with a game they made themselves.

What your child takes away

  • Their first working code and a simple game
  • The idea that errors are puzzles, not failures
  • Confidence that coding is something they can do
  • A certificate of participation

Who it suits

Children aged 8 to 12 who love games, puzzles or building things — including complete beginners who have never written code. A laptop or desktop computer is best for this workshop.

Want more after Code Quest?

The full Fun with Coding course (ages 10–16) continues with loops, variables and conditions, and older students can move on to Learn Python. Try our free robot sandwich instructions activity before the day.

How to register

Registration is personal for now — there is no online form yet. Send your child's name, age and the workshop through our contact page or on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. We confirm the place and send the Zoom joining details before the day. Places are kept small so every child gets attention, so it is worth registering early.

What you need on the day

  • A laptop, tablet or computer with a stable internet connection
  • The Zoom app installed beforehand
  • A quiet spot, plus a notebook and pencil
  • For younger children, a parent nearby for the first few minutes

After the workshop

Children who attend the full workshop receive a certificate of participation. If your child enjoyed it, we will tell you about the matching course and you can book a free demo class — nothing is automatic, and there is no obligation to enrol.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need coding experience?
No. Code Quest is designed for complete beginners.

Can my child join on a tablet?
A laptop or desktop computer works best for coding. Message us if you only have a tablet and we will advise.

Is the workshop really free?
Yes. It is free this season; it normally costs PKR 1,500 per child. No card or payment is needed to register.

How do we register?
Send your child's name, age and the workshop through the contact page or on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. We confirm the place and send the Zoom details.

Can my child join more than one workshop?
Each child can take part in one workshop this season. Pick the one your child is most excited about.
