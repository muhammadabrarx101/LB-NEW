Free IGCSE and O Level demo class

Thirty minutes with a subject specialist: a short topic diagnostic, a slice of exam-focused teaching and an honest view of the grade the student can reach.

Book this free demo

What happens in the 30 minutes

  1. Subject, board and exam session — so we teach to the right syllabus.
  2. A short topic diagnostic to find where marks are being lost.
  3. Exam-focused teaching on one weak topic, using past-paper style questions.
  4. An honest outlook on the grade and the time needed.

Subjects

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths, English, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies, Accounting, Economics and Business Studies. See all IGCSE and O Level subjects.

What to prepare

  • A laptop or computer with a camera and headphones
  • The syllabus code if you know it (it is on the statement of entry)
  • A recent test or mock result, if you have one

The demo is a slice of our IGCSE Academics programme.

What happens after the demo

You get an honest view of where the student is and what would help — including if we are not the right fit. If you want to continue, we share schedules, group options and fees on WhatsApp. If not, that is the end of it: no card details, no payment and no follow-up pressure.

How to book

Use the booking button on this page, or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. Tell us the student's age and which days and times suit you, and we will reply with two or three slots — usually within a few hours. See all free demo classes.

Frequently asked questions

Which subjects can I book an IGCSE demo for?
Any of the core IGCSE and O Level subjects we teach, including the sciences, Maths, English, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies, Accounting, Economics and Business Studies.

Can I book demos for two subjects?
Yes. Mention both and we will arrange separate short demos rather than squeezing two subjects into one.

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. It is free, takes about 30 minutes and needs no card details or payment.

Who teaches the demo?
The teacher who would actually take the course, so you see the real teaching style.
