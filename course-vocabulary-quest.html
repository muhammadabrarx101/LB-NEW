Vocabulary Quest

A bigger vocabulary, without the flashcard grind.

Ages 14–1810 sessions · 1.5 monthsIntermediateLive online · small group

About this course

A dynamic course designed to strengthen vocabulary, comprehension and expression. Students learn advanced, context-based words through games, challenges and real-life usage — helping them speak and write with clarity and confidence.

What is included

  • Words learned in context, not in lists
  • Game and challenge-based sessions
  • Direct payoff for essays and exams
  • Comprehension and expression together

How it runs

Live online · small group — taught live, never pre-recorded, with sessions recorded afterwards so students can revisit anything they missed. Timings are arranged around school and time zones, and we reschedule when exams or travel get in the way.

Learning Bubble teaches online to students across Pakistan — Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad — as well as the Gulf, the UK and North America. You will need a laptop or tablet with a working camera and a reasonably stable connection. If a course requires any materials at home, we send the list before the first session.
