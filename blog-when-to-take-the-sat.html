When should you take the SAT? A timeline for Grades 10 to 12

Most students should sit the SAT for the first time in Grade 11, with a second attempt before applications open. Here is how to plan backwards from your deadlines.

Quick answer

For most students the best plan is a first SAT in Grade 11, after three to four months of preparation, and a second attempt in late Grade 11 or early Grade 12 — leaving enough time for scores to reach universities before application deadlines.

Work backwards from your deadlines

US application deadlines commonly fall between November and January of Grade 12, with early deadlines in November. Count back:

  1. Your final test date needs to be early enough for scores to be sent before the deadline.
  2. Your first attempt should be a few months earlier, leaving room to improve.
  3. Preparation should start three to four months before the first attempt.

A sample timeline

WhenWhat to do
Grade 10Strengthen Maths and reading; no need to sit the SAT yet
Early Grade 11Diagnostic test; begin structured preparation
Mid–late Grade 11First SAT attempt
Summer before Grade 12Targeted work on weak areas
Early Grade 12Second attempt, if needed

How many attempts?

Two or three is common. Many universities superscore, combining your best section scores across dates. Beyond three attempts, gains usually become small, and the time is better spent on essays and applications.

Fit it around school exams

Students in Pakistan often juggle the SAT with O Level, IGCSE or A-Level exams. Avoid sitting the SAT in the weeks just before a major exam session. Plan the SAT for a period when school pressure is lighter.

Register early

The SAT is offered on several dates each year at test centres in Pakistan. Popular centres fill up, and late registration may cost more or be unavailable. Check dates on the College Board website and book well ahead.

Start with a diagnostic

The most useful first step is a full, timed official practice test. It tells you where you are, how far you need to go, and whether three months or six is the realistic preparation window.

Frequently asked questions

What grade should I take the SAT in?
Most students take it for the first time in Grade 11, then once more in late Grade 11 or early Grade 12 if needed.

How many times can I take the SAT?
There is no strict limit. Two or three attempts is common, and many universities superscore.

How long should I prepare for the SAT?
Three to four months of structured preparation suits most students. A diagnostic test shows whether you need more or less.

Where can I take the SAT in Pakistan?
At authorised test centres in several Pakistani cities. Check current dates and centres on the College Board website.
