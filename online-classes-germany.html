Online classes in English for families in Germany

Expat and international-school families in Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg and across Germany learn with Learning Bubble live online, in English.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble teaches students in Germany — Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Düsseldorf and Cologne — through live online classes taught in English. We mainly support international-school students taking IGCSE and A-Level, students preparing for IELTS or the SAT, children who want English, coding, AI or creative writing in English, and families who have moved to Germany and want to keep a child's English strong. Pakistan is 3 to 4 hours ahead of Germany. Start with a free demo class.

Why families choose Learning Bubble

  • Live teachers, never pre-recorded videos — with recordings after class for revision
  • Small groups of 3–5, or one-to-one — every student speaks and gets feedback
  • All classes taught in English by the Learning Bubble team
  • Timed for German afternoons and weekends — agreed when you enrol

For international-school and expat families

Many international schools in Germany teach IGCSE and A-Level in English. Our IGCSE tuition and A-Level tuition follow the major international boards, with past-paper practice and exam technique. For families who mostly speak German or another language at home, our English courses and Vocabulary Quest keep a child's English growing.

Frequently asked questions

Are the classes taught in German?
No. All Learning Bubble classes are taught in English. They suit international-school students, expat families and anyone who wants to learn in English.

What time are classes for students in Germany?
Pakistan is 3 to 4 hours ahead of Germany, depending on daylight saving. We arrange afternoon, evening and weekend slots in German time.

Do you teach the German Abitur?
No. For exams we teach IGCSE, A-Level, IELTS and SAT, which are common at international schools in Germany.

How much do the classes cost?
Fees depend on the course, small group or one-to-one, and the number of sessions, so we quote them personally. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610 or book a free demo for the exact fee.

Is there a free trial class?
Yes. Every course starts with a free 30-minute demo class, with no obligation to enrol.
