StoryCraft: free creative writing workshop for kids (ages 7–10)

A free, live creative writing workshop on Zoom where children invent a character, build a world around them and write the opening of their own story.

Register free

Many children love stories but freeze in front of a blank page. StoryCraft is built around that moment. Instead of being told to "write a story", children build one piece at a time — so by the time they start writing, they already know who their story is about and where it happens.

What happens in the workshop

  1. Meet the group. A quick, friendly warm-up so everyone feels comfortable speaking.
  2. Invent a character. Every child creates a character with a goal, a flaw and a secret — the three things that make readers care.
  3. Build their world. Using all five senses, children describe where the story happens so it feels real.
  4. Write the opening. With guidance, they write the first lines of their story — an opening that makes a reader want more.
  5. Share. Children who want to read aloud share with the small group and hear what worked.

What your child takes away

  • A character and setting they invented themselves
  • The opening of a story they can keep writing at home
  • Confidence that the blank page is not so scary
  • A certificate of participation

Who it suits

Children aged 7 to 10 who like books, make up games or characters, or find school writing tasks a chore. No writing experience is needed.

Want more after StoryCraft?

The full Creative Writing course for ages 8–10 runs all year, and our free character builder activity is a great warm-up before the day.

How to register

Fill in our short online registration form (it takes about two minutes), or send your child's name, age and the workshop on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. We confirm the place and send the Zoom joining details before the day. Places are kept small so every child gets attention, so it is worth registering early.

What you need on the day

  • A laptop, tablet or computer with a stable internet connection
  • The Zoom app installed beforehand
  • A quiet spot, plus a notebook and pencil
  • For younger children, a parent nearby for the first few minutes

After the workshop

Children who attend the full workshop receive a certificate of participation. If your child enjoyed it, we will tell you about the matching course and you can book a free demo class — nothing is automatic, and there is no obligation to enrol.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need to be a good writer?
No. StoryCraft is designed for beginners. Children build ideas step by step before writing, so nobody is left staring at a blank page.

Will my child have to read aloud?
Only if they want to. Sharing is encouraged but never forced.

Is the workshop really free?
Yes. It is free this season; it normally costs PKR 1,500 per child. No card or payment is needed to register.

How do we register?
Fill in the online registration form linked on this page, or send your child's name, age and the workshop on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. We confirm the place and send the Zoom details.

Can my child join more than one workshop?
Each child can take part in one workshop this season. Pick the one your child is most excited about.
