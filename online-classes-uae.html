Online tutoring in Dubai and the UAE — IGCSE, A-Level, IELTS and kids courses

Families in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and across the UAE learn with Learning Bubble live online, one hour behind Pakistan time, in small groups or one-to-one.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble teaches students across the UAE — Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman and Al Ain — through live online classes. We offer IGCSE and A-Level tuition, IELTS and SAT preparation, English courses, and coding, AI and creative writing for kids. The UAE is one hour behind Pakistan, so after-school and weekend slots are easy to arrange. Start with a free demo class.

Why UAE families choose online tutoring

  • No driving across Dubai in evening traffic for a single lesson
  • Small groups of 3–5, or one-to-one — more attention than a crowded tuition centre
  • Live, never pre-recorded — with recordings afterwards for revision
  • Timings in UAE time — afternoons, evenings and weekends, agreed when you enrol

IGCSE and A-Level tutoring for British-curriculum schools

Many UAE schools follow the British curriculum, so IGCSE and A-Level support is our most requested academics service there:

  • IGCSE tuition — Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Economics, Business and Accounting
  • A-Level tuition — AS and A2 across sciences, maths and commerce

Every student starts with a short diagnostic, so lessons target the topics where marks are being lost. See all IGCSE subjects we teach.

IELTS and SAT preparation in the UAE

  • IELTS Academic for university admission in the UK, Canada and Australia
  • IELTS General Training for work and migration
  • SAT preparation for US universities

Both tests can be sat in the UAE; we prepare students online, and they book the test locally.

Coding, AI and creative courses for kids in the UAE

  • AI classes for kids — how AI works and how to use it safely
  • Creative writing, poetry and storytelling
