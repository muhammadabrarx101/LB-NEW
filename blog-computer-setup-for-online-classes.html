What your child needs for online coding and live classes

A smooth first session starts before class. Here is the setup checklist we send families — the device, the connection, the space and the small things that help.

Quick answer

For most live classes your child needs a laptop or desktop computer, a stable internet connection, headphones with a microphone, a working camera and a quiet space. For coding, a laptop or desktop is strongly recommended over a tablet or phone.

The device

ActivityRecommendedWorkable
Coding classesLaptop or desktop—
Design classesLaptop or desktopTablet for some tools
Writing and discussion classesLaptop or desktopTablet
Free workshops on ZoomLaptop or desktopTablet

Coding needs a proper keyboard and the ability to install software, which phones and most tablets cannot do well.

Internet

A stable connection matters more than raw speed. If the connection is unreliable:

  • Sit close to the router
  • Ask others at home to pause heavy downloads during class
  • Keep a mobile hotspot as a backup
  • Turn off the camera briefly if video freezes

Audio and video

  • Headphones with a microphone reduce echo and help children focus
  • A working camera helps the teacher see when a child is stuck
  • Test both before the first class

Software

  • Install the video app (often Zoom) in advance
  • For coding courses, install the course software using the setup guide we send before session one
  • Keep the device updated and charged

The space

  • A quiet room with few distractions
  • A table and chair, not a bed
  • Good light in front of the child, not behind
  • Water and a notebook nearby

For younger children

Sit with them for the first few minutes to help with sound and camera, then step back. Most children settle quickly once they see the teacher and other students.

Tips for the first session

  1. Join five minutes early
  2. Close other apps and tabs
  3. Keep notifications off
  4. Have the setup guide open in case anything needs checking

We send a setup guide before every course, and you can always message us on WhatsApp if something is not working.

Frequently asked questions

Can my child do coding classes on a tablet?
A laptop or desktop is strongly recommended for coding, because it needs a keyboard and installed software.

What internet speed do online classes need?
A stable connection matters more than speed. Sit near the router and pause heavy downloads during class.

Does my child need a camera for online classes?
It helps the teacher see when a child is stuck and keeps the class interactive, so a working camera is recommended.

What software is needed for coding classes?
It depends on the course. We send a setup guide with links and instructions before the first session.
