Online classes in English for students in China

International-school and expat families in Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou and across China learn with Learning Bubble live online.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble teaches students in China — Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Hong Kong — through live online classes in English. Our classes suit students who attend international schools and want help with IGCSE and A-Level, or are preparing for IELTS or the SAT to study abroad. Younger children join our English, coding, AI and creative writing courses. China is 3 hours ahead of Pakistan, so after-school slots in China fall in the Pakistani afternoon. Start with a free demo class.

Why families choose Learning Bubble

  • Live teachers, never pre-recorded videos — with recordings after class for revision
  • Small groups of 3–5, or one-to-one — every student speaks and gets feedback
  • All classes taught in English by the Learning Bubble team
  • Timed for China Standard Time — weekday evenings and weekends

Study-abroad preparation

Students in China applying to universities in the UK, the US, Canada or Australia usually need an English test and sometimes the SAT. Our IELTS Academic course builds band-score strategy with mock tests, and our SAT preparation course follows a structured plan built around a target score. See what IELTS band you need and what a good SAT score is.

Frequently asked questions

Are classes taught in English?
Yes. All classes are taught in English, which suits international-school students and anyone preparing for IELTS, the SAT or study abroad.

What time are classes for students in China?
China is 3 hours ahead of Pakistan, so we arrange weekday evening and weekend slots in China Standard Time.

Do you teach the Gaokao?
No. We teach IGCSE, A-Level, IELTS and SAT, which are used by international schools and for study abroad.

How much do the classes cost?
Fees depend on the course, small group or one-to-one, and the number of sessions, so we quote them personally. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610 or book a free demo for the exact fee.

Is there a free trial class?
Yes. Every course starts with a free 30-minute demo class, with no obligation to enrol.
