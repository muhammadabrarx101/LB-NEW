SAT in Pakistan 2026: fees, registration and test centres

A step-by-step guide for Pakistani students: what the SAT costs, how to register, where you can sit it, and how to pick a date that fits your university deadlines.

Quick answer

For students in Pakistan, the SAT costs USD 111: the USD 68 registration fee plus a USD 43 international fee. Late registration adds USD 38, and changing your test centre or cancelling after the deadline costs extra. You register and pay online through College Board, and you sit the digital SAT at an approved test centre.

You pay in US dollars by card, so the final rupee amount depends on your bank's exchange rate, and some banks add card charges or advance tax. These figures were published for 2026; College Board can change them, so check the official fee page when you register.

How to register for the SAT from Pakistan

  1. Create a College Board account using your name exactly as it appears on your passport.
  2. Choose a test date and centre. Search for centres in your city and register early, because seats fill up.
  3. Upload a photo that meets College Board's requirements.
  4. Pay the fee online by card.
  5. Install Bluebook, College Board's testing app, on the device you will use, and complete the practice test in it.
  6. Print or save your admission ticket and bring an acceptable ID, usually your passport.

SAT test centres in Pakistan

The SAT is offered at approved centres in major cities, including Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, usually at schools that host international testing. Centres and seats change every year, so use the test-centre search on College Board when you register. If your city has no centre, plan travel early.

Choosing your SAT test date

International SAT dates usually fall in August, October, November, December, March, May and June. Work backwards from your application deadlines:

  • Early applications (November deadlines): aim to have a good score by October
  • Regular decision (January deadlines): November or December is usually the last practical date
  • Leave room for a second attempt. Many students improve on a retake

Frequently asked questions

How much is the SAT fee in Pakistan?
USD 111 in total for most students: the USD 68 registration fee plus a USD 43 international fee. Late registration adds USD 38. You pay online in dollars, so the rupee amount depends on your bank.

Where can I take the SAT in Pakistan?
At approved test centres in major cities such as Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad. Use the College Board test-centre search when you register, because centres and seats change.

Is the SAT in Pakistan on paper or digital?
Digital. Students take the SAT in College Board's Bluebook app at a test centre.

How many times can I take the SAT?
There is no strict limit, and many students take it two or three times. Most universities consider your best score, and some combine section scores.

When should I register for the SAT?
As early as possible, ideally two to three months before your chosen date, because popular centres fill up and late registration costs extra.
