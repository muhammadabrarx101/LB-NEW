Free IELTS demo class

Thirty minutes with an IELTS teacher: a short diagnostic, a focused piece of teaching on your weakest module, and an honest timeline to your target band.

Book this free demo

What happens in the 30 minutes

  1. Your target and deadline — band, purpose (study or migration) and test date.
  2. A short diagnostic, focused on the module you are most worried about — often Writing or Speaking.
  3. A real slice of teaching on that module.
  4. An honest estimate of how long preparation will realistically take.

Who it suits

Candidates for IELTS Academic (university study) or General Training (migration and work). Not sure which? Read IELTS Academic vs General Training.

What to prepare

  • A laptop or computer with a camera and headphones
  • If you have one, a recent piece of writing or a past score
  • Your target band and deadline

The demo is a slice of our IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training programmes.

What happens after the demo

You get an honest view of where the student is and what would help — including if we are not the right fit. If you want to continue, we share schedules, group options and fees on WhatsApp. If not, that is the end of it: no card details, no payment and no follow-up pressure.

How to book

Use the booking button on this page, or message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610. Tell us the student's age and which days and times suit you, and we will reply with two or three slots — usually within a few hours. See all free demo classes.

Frequently asked questions

Will I get a band estimate in the demo?
You will get an honest indication of where you are and how far from your target, based on a short diagnostic. A full diagnostic comes at the start of the course.

Is the demo for Academic or General Training?
Either. Tell us which test you need when you book.

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. It is free, takes about 30 minutes and needs no card details or payment.

Who teaches the demo?
The teacher who would actually take the course, so you see the real teaching style.
