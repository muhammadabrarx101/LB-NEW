15 coding project ideas for kids, from beginner to intermediate

Children learn coding fastest when they are building something they care about. Here are fifteen projects that work, and what each one teaches.

Quick answer

The best coding projects are small, finishable and personal. Start with drawings and quizzes, move to simple games, then try projects that use data. Each project below lists the main concept it teaches.

Beginner projects

ProjectWhat it teaches
1. Draw your initials or a house with codeCoordinates and sequences
2. A colourful spiral patternLoops
3. "Guess my favourite animal" quizInput and conditions
4. Pocket-money calculatorVariables and arithmetic
5. Times-table printerLoops with numbers

Intermediate projects

ProjectWhat it teaches
6. Guess-the-number gameLoops, conditions and random numbers
7. Rock, paper, scissorsConditions and comparing values
8. A maths quiz that keeps scoreVariables, loops and conditions together
9. A simple animationMovement, timing and loops
10. A text adventure storyBranching choices and functions

Stretch projects

ProjectWhat it teaches
11. To-do list appLists and adding or removing items
12. Vocabulary flashcard testerLists or dictionaries and random choice
13. Class survey results chartData and simple visualisation
14. A simple catch-the-object gameEvents, collision and scoring
15. A weather or sports data reportReading data and summarising it

How to make a project succeed

  1. Start smaller than you think. A working small version beats an unfinished big one.
  2. Make it personal. Their name, their favourite colours, their friends in the quiz.
  3. Add one feature at a time, testing after each.
  4. Expect bugs. Debugging is part of coding, not a sign of failure.
  5. Show someone. Sharing a finished project builds pride and motivation.

Where to start

Projects 1–5 suit beginners in visual or simple text languages; 6–10 suit students comfortable with loops and conditions; 11–15 suit students learning Python. Want to try coding without a computer first? See our free coding activities for kids.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good first coding project for a child?
Something small and visual, such as drawing their initials or a spiral pattern with code, or a simple quiz.

How long should a kids' coding project take?
A first project should be finishable in one session. Later projects can grow over several sessions, one feature at a time.

What projects can kids build in Python?
Quizzes, guess-the-number games, text adventures, flashcard testers and simple data charts.

My child gets frustrated by bugs. What helps?
Smaller steps, testing after each change, and treating bugs as puzzles. A teacher who explains errors also helps a lot.
