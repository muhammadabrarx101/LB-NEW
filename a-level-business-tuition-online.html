A Level Business tuition online

Live online A Level Business classes for Cambridge 9609: business theory applied to case studies, the calculations examiners expect, and evaluation that earns top marks.

A Level Business looks easy until the exam. The theory is approachable, but the marks come from applying it to the case in front of you, doing the calculations accurately, and reaching a justified decision. Students who write general answers that could fit any business rarely get the top grades.

Most of our A Level students sit Cambridge International AS & A Level. If your child is on a different board, such as Pearson Edexcel International A Level, tell us the units and we will confirm honestly whether we can help.

What the syllabus covers

  • Business and its environment: enterprise, business structure, size, objectives, stakeholders and the external environment
  • Human resource management: motivation, leadership, management and workforce planning
  • Marketing: market research, the marketing mix and marketing strategy
  • Operations management: operations, inventory, capacity and quality
  • Finance and accounting: sources of finance, costs, budgets, financial statements and ratio analysis
  • Strategic management (A2): strategic analysis, choice and implementation

Assessment includes short-answer and essay questions, data response, and longer case study papers at A2.

How we teach A Level Business

Every student starts with a topic-by-topic diagnostic. "Weak at Business" is not specific enough to fix: we need to know which topics lose marks, and whether the problem is understanding, recall or exam technique.

  1. Diagnose: a short test across the specification, marked against real Cambridge mark schemes.
  2. Rebuild concepts: we fix the underlying idea first, because technique built on a shaky concept collapses under exam pressure.
  3. Past-paper cycles: topic-wise questions first, then full papers, learning the command words and exactly what examiners credit.
  4. Timed practice and tracking: full papers under exam timing in the final weeks, with progress tracked topic by topic.

Classes are live online in groups of 3 to 5 students, or one-to-one when a student is starting late, retaking or has very specific gaps. Sessions are recorded for revision, and parents get honest progress updates on WhatsApp. We teach students across Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and beyond), the Gulf, the UK and North America.

Where students usually lose marks

  • Answers not applied to the case: generic points that could fit any business
  • Calculations: ratios, break-even, investment appraisal and budget variances
  • Evaluation that does not weigh options or reach a decision
  • Time management on long case study papers
  • Using business terms precisely, such as profit versus cash flow
