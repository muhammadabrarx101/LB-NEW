Online SAT tutoring and kids classes for families in the USA

Families across the United States learn with Learning Bubble live online — SAT prep for teens, and coding, AI and creative writing for younger learners.

Quick answer

Learning Bubble offers online SAT tutoring and coding, AI, Python and creative writing classes for kids to families in the United States. Classes are live, in small groups or one-to-one, scheduled at US-friendly times such as weekend mornings and weekday evenings. Start with a free demo class.

Online SAT tutoring

Our SAT preparation course follows a structured plan for the digital SAT, built around the student's target score:

  • A diagnostic first, so practice goes where points are being lost
  • Reading and Writing strategy, and Math practice including Desmos
  • Timed practice tests, reviewed question by question
  • One-to-one SAT tutoring available alongside small groups

Frequently asked questions

What time are classes for students in the USA?
Pakistan is 9 to 13 hours ahead of US time zones, so US students usually join on weekend mornings or weekday evenings in their own time zone. Exact slots are agreed when you enrol.

Do you offer one-on-one SAT tutoring?
Yes. SAT preparation is available one-to-one as well as in small groups of three to five students.

Are classes taught in English?
Yes. All Learning Bubble classes are taught in English by live teachers.

How much do the classes cost?
Fees depend on the course, small group or one-to-one, and the number of sessions, so we quote them personally. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 321 2481610 or book a free demo for the exact fee.

Is there a free trial class?
Yes. Every course starts with a free 30-minute demo class, with no obligation to enrol.
