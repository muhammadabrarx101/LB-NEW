IELTS Listening tips: format, question types and easy marks

Listening is the module that improves fastest with practice. Most lost marks are not about understanding — they are about spelling, word limits and losing your place.

Quick answer

IELTS Listening has four parts and 40 questions and takes about 30 minutes. You hear each recording once. It is the same in Academic and General Training.

The four parts

PartSituationTypical example
1Everyday conversationBooking a room, registering for a course
2Everyday monologueA tour guide or a radio announcement
3Academic conversationStudents discussing an assignment with a tutor
4Academic monologueA short lecture

The parts get harder as the test goes on, so Part 4 is usually where higher bands are won or lost.

Common question types

  • Form, note, table and sentence completion
  • Multiple choice
  • Matching
  • Plan, map or diagram labelling

The easy marks people throw away

  • Spelling. A misspelled answer is a wrong answer. Practise spelling common names, places, days and months.
  • Word limits. If the instruction says no more than two words, three words is wrong — even if the meaning is right.
  • Plurals. If the speaker says tickets, writing ticket can cost the mark.
  • Numbers and dates. Practise hearing the difference between fifteen and fifty.
  • Losing your place. If you miss an answer, move on immediately rather than missing the next three.

Before each section

You get time to read the questions before each recording. Use it to underline keywords and predict the type of answer — a number, a name, a noun, a place. Prediction is the most useful Listening skill there is.

Paper or computer?

On paper, you get extra time at the end to transfer answers to the answer sheet. On computer, you type answers as you go and get a shorter check at the end. Practise in the format you will actually sit.

How to practise

Use real practice tests under timed conditions, then go back through the transcript to find exactly why each wrong answer was wrong. Two weeks of this, done carefully, usually moves the Listening band noticeably.

Frequently asked questions

How many times is the IELTS Listening recording played?
Once only, so prediction and keeping your place are essential skills.

Does spelling count in IELTS Listening?
Yes. An answer with a spelling mistake is marked wrong.

Is IELTS Listening the same for Academic and General Training?
Yes. The Listening test is identical in both versions.

How many correct answers do I need for band 7?
Roughly 30 to 31 out of 40, although the conversion can vary slightly between test versions.
