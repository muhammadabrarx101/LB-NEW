Tech careers explained for teens: what people actually do

"Working in tech" covers dozens of very different jobs. Knowing what they involve helps teenagers choose subjects and skills with a purpose.

Quick answer

Tech careers include software development, data analysis and data science, AI and machine learning, design, cybersecurity, IT support and product management. Most value problem-solving, communication and continuous learning as much as specific coding skills.

A quick map of tech careers

CareerWhat they actually doHelpful subjects
Software developerBuild apps, websites and systemsMaths, computer science
Data analyst / scientistFind answers in dataMaths, statistics, computing
AI / machine learning engineerBuild systems that learn from dataMaths, computer science
UX / UI designerDesign how apps look and feelArt, design, psychology
Cybersecurity specialistProtect systems from attackComputing, maths
IT support / systemsKeep technology runningComputing
Product managerDecide what gets built, and whyBusiness, communication

Skills that matter across all of them

  • Problem-solving — breaking problems into steps
  • Communication — explaining ideas to people who are not technical
  • Learning continuously — tools change constantly
  • Teamwork — almost all tech work is collaborative

What teenagers can do now

  1. Learn a programming language, such as Python
  2. Build small projects and keep them as a portfolio
  3. Take maths seriously — it opens most technical paths
  4. Try design if you enjoy the visual side — see Graphic Design
  5. Understand AI and how it is changing jobs — see AI for Kids

Do you need a computer science degree?

Many tech roles are filled by computer science graduates, but people also arrive from maths, engineering, physics, design and business — and some through self-taught portfolios. Strong skills and evidence of projects matter a great deal.

Not sure yet? That is normal

Most teenagers do not know what they want to do, and that is fine. Building general skills — coding, clear thinking, communication — keeps many doors open.

Frequently asked questions

What tech careers are there?
Software development, data analysis and data science, AI and machine learning, design, cybersecurity, IT support and product management, among others.

What subjects should I take for a tech career?
Maths and computer science help most technical roles. Design, business and communication help in others.

Do I need to code to work in tech?
Not for every role. Design, product and some business roles need less coding, but basic coding knowledge helps in almost all of them.

How can teenagers prepare for a tech career?
Learn a language like Python, build small projects, take maths seriously and keep learning as tools change.
