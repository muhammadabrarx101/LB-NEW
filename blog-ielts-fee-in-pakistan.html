IELTS fee in Pakistan 2026: costs, booking and refunds

What the IELTS test costs in Pakistan right now, which version you actually need, how to book and pay, and what happens if you cancel or move your date.

Quick answer

As of October 2026, standard IELTS Academic or General Training on computer costs PKR 75,600 in most cities and PKR 76,240 in Lahore and several other Punjab cities. IELTS for UKVI costs PKR 78,820 and IELTS Life Skills costs PKR 53,000. Fees change, so always confirm the final price on the official British Council or IDP booking page before you pay.

IELTS fees in Pakistan at a glance

TestFee (PKR)Who it is for
IELTS Academic (computer)75,600 to 76,240University admission abroad, professional registration
IELTS General Training (computer)75,600 to 76,240Work, migration and PR applications
IELTS for UKVI (Academic or GT)78,820UK visa routes that require a UKVI-approved test
IELTS Life Skills (A1, A2, B1)53,000Some UK family, settlement and citizenship routes

Figures are based on the British Council pricing effective July 2026 and IDP's published fee table, as reported in October 2026. The price can differ slightly by city and venue.

Which IELTS do you actually need?

This matters more than the fee, because the tests are not interchangeable:

  • Studying abroad? Most universities want IELTS Academic. If the course is in the UK and your visa route needs it, you may need IELTS for UKVI (Academic).
  • Migrating or working abroad? Usually IELTS General Training. Canada, Australia and the UK each publish which tests they accept.
  • UK family or settlement visa? Some routes need IELTS Life Skills at a set level.

Not sure between the two main versions? Read IELTS Academic vs General Training: which one do you need?

How to book IELTS in Pakistan

  1. Confirm the exact test your university, employer or visa route requires.
  2. Choose a provider and city. Both the British Council and IDP run IELTS in Pakistan's major cities, including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
  3. Register online and pick a date. Computer-delivered tests have frequent dates.
  4. Pay within the deadline. The British Council asks for payment within 3 days of registering. Online card payment, bank transfer and bank deposit are common options; check your provider's page for current methods.
  5. Bring your passport or CNIC as instructed. Your ID must match your booking exactly.

Paying by card? Your bank may add exchange-rate differences or advance tax, so the amount on your statement can be slightly higher than the listed fee.

Cancelling or changing your IELTS date

For standard IELTS, the general rules reported in 2026 are:

When you cancel or transferWhat happens
15 or more days before the testCancel: 75% refund. Transfer: usually free
5 to 14 days beforeNo normal refund. Transfer: a fee of up to 25%
Within 4 daysUsually treated as a cancellation

UKVI and Life Skills tests have their own refund scale. Medical emergencies can qualify for special consideration with evidence, but approval is not automatic.

Make the fee count

At over PKR 75,000, a retake is expensive. The cheapest IELTS is the one you only take once. Before booking:

  • Take a full timed practice test to see your real band
  • Check every module against your target, not just the overall score

Frequently asked questions

What is the IELTS fee in Pakistan in 2026?
As of October 2026, IELTS Academic or General Training on computer costs PKR 75,600 in most cities and PKR 76,240 in some Punjab cities. IELTS for UKVI costs PKR 78,820 and IELTS Life Skills PKR 53,000. Confirm the current fee with the British Council or IDP before paying.

Is the IELTS fee the same at British Council and IDP?
Published fees for the main tests are very similar, but the exact amount can vary by city and venue. Check the final price on the booking page.

Can I get a refund if I cancel my IELTS test?
For standard IELTS, cancelling 15 or more days before the test usually gets a 75% refund. Closer to the date, you normally cannot get a refund except through special consideration, for example a documented medical emergency.

Is IELTS on paper still available in Pakistan?
Standard IELTS in Pakistan is now delivered on computer. Paper-based tests are generally reserved for candidates with approved special arrangements.

How much is the IELTS One Skill Retake?
IDP lists it at around PKR 48,000. It lets you retake one module after a computer-delivered test, but not every institution accepts it, so check first.
